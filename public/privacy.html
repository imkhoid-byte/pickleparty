Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PickleParty ("we") makes a pickleball scorekeeping and party game app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Team names, scores, game history, custom cards and settings are saved only on your device. We do not upload them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple (App Store), Google (Google Play) or, on the web, Stripe. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, not your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Waitlist

If you join the Pro waitlist, we store your email address with our host (Netlify) and use it only to tell you about PickleParty. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

PickleParty is not directed at children under 13. Drinking-game modes are only for adults of legal drinking age.
